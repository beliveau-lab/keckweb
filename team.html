Meet Our Team

The Keck Microscopy Center is staffed by experienced professionals dedicated to supporting your research through cutting-edge microscopy and imaging expertise.

Technical Staff

Contact Our Team

For inquiries about our services, equipment availability, training, or collaboration opportunities, please contact us. Our team is here to help support your research needs.

Collaborations

We actively collaborate with researchers from various departments and institutions. If you're interested in partnering with the Keck Microscopy Center or have questions about collaborative research opportunities, don't hesitate to reach out.
