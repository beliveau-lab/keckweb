Latest News

Stay informed about the latest developments, equipment updates, and announcements from the W. M. Keck Microscopy Center.

📅 September 2024

New Zeiss 880 Confocal Microscope Available

We are pleased to announce that our state-of-the-art Zeiss 880 Confocal Microscope is now fully operational and available for booking. This advanced imaging system offers exceptional resolution and speed for your research needs.

📅 August 2024

Extended Summer Hours

The Keck Center will be extending its operating hours through August to accommodate summer research activities. Please check our schedule page for updated operating hours and availability.

📅 July 2024

Advanced Imaging Workshop

Join us for our upcoming Advanced Imaging Workshop covering the latest techniques in confocal microscopy, live-cell imaging, and image analysis. Registration is now open for all UW researchers.

📅 June 2024

New Image Analysis Software

We have added IMARIS advanced image analysis software to our facility. Training sessions are available for all users. Contact us to schedule your training appointment.
