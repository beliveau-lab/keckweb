About the Center

The W. M. Keck Microscopy Center provides UW researchers with access to cutting-edge light microscopy equipment, expert technical support, and comprehensive training. We serve researchers across Physiology & Biophysics, Pharmacology, Genome Sciences, Laboratory Medicine, and many other departments.
