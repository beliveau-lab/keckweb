About

Advancing research
through imaging

The W. M. Keck Microscopy Center was established in 1990 through a W. M. Keck Foundation grant and has grown into a shared facility serving the broader UW research community. We support researchers across Physiology & Biophysics, Pharmacology, Genome Sciences, Laboratory Medicine & Pathology, and many other departments.

Grant acknowledgement

All publications using data from the Leica SP8X Confocal must acknowledge NIH grant S10 OD016240. The IMARIS workstation and SP8X scan head electronics were funded by the UW Student Technology Fee.
