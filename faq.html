Frequently Asked Questions

Below are answers to some of the most common questions we receive. If you can't find what you're looking for, please contact us directly.

General Information

What are the center's hours of operation? +

The Keck Microscopy Center is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Facility access outside of these hours may be available for approved users. Please contact us to arrange after-hours access.

Is the facility open to external users? +

Yes, the Keck Microscopy Center welcomes collaborators from other institutions. We can accommodate external researchers for imaging projects. Please contact us to discuss your research needs and availability.

How do I book equipment? +

Equipment can be booked through our online calendar system. First-time users should contact our team for training and account setup. View our available equipment and booking information.

Equipment & Imaging

What imaging techniques are supported? +

We support a wide range of imaging techniques including:

  • Confocal microscopy (Leica SP8X)
  • Widefield microscopy (Leica Inverted)
  • Multi-photon imaging
  • Live-cell imaging with temperature control
  • High-resolution image analysis with IMARIS
Can I bring my own samples? +

Yes, we accept user-prepared samples. We recommend consulting with our imaging specialists before bringing samples to ensure they are compatible with our equipment and imaging protocols. Some samples may require special handling or preparation.

What file formats are supported for data storage? +

Our microscopy systems generate data in multiple formats including TIFF, LZW-compressed TIFF, and proprietary formats (LIF for Leica). All data can be exported to standard formats compatible with analysis software. Raw data files can be stored on USB drives or transferred via our data management system.

Training & Support

Is training required to use the equipment? +

Yes, all users must complete training before operating equipment independently. Our training coordinator provides comprehensive instruction tailored to your specific needs. Training sessions typically last 1-2 hours and cover equipment operation, image acquisition, and best practices.

Can you help with image analysis? +

Yes, our image analysis team can assist with processing, quantification, and interpretation of your microscopy data. We use industry-standard software including IMARIS, Fiji, and custom analysis workflows. Consultation is available to discuss your analytical needs.

Can you provide consultation on experimental design? +

Absolutely. Our experienced staff can advise on imaging strategies, optimal sample preparation, and experimental design to achieve your research goals. We encourage early consultation to ensure your experiments are optimized for success.

Policies & Fees

What is the cost of using the facility? +

Equipment rental rates and facility fees vary depending on the equipment and user status. Academic users typically receive discounted rates. Please contact us for a detailed pricing structure and information about cost-sharing options.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Reservations can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled time without penalty. Cancellations within 24 hours may result in charges depending on circumstances. Please refer to our facility policies for complete details.

What happens if equipment malfunctions during my session? +

We maintain rigorous equipment maintenance schedules to prevent issues. If equipment malfunctions occur during your session, we will work to resolve the issue promptly. If the equipment cannot be repaired, your booking can be rescheduled at no charge or your fees will be refunded.
