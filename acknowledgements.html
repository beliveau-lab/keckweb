Acknowledgements

Recognizing the people and institutions that support the Keck Center

Supporting the Keck Microscopy Center

The W. M. Keck Microscopy Center would not be possible without the generous support and collaboration of many individuals and institutions. We are grateful for:

🙏 Core Support

We acknowledge the foundational support from the W. M. Keck Foundation, which made the establishment and continued operation of this facility possible.

Partner Departments

Our facility is supported by and serves researchers from the following University of Washington departments:

Department of Physiology & Biophysics

Leading contributors to advanced microscopy research and training programs.

Department of Pharmacology

Active users and supporters of cutting-edge imaging methodologies for drug discovery research.

Department of Genome Sciences

Driving innovation in molecular imaging and biological visualization techniques.

Department of Laboratory Medicine & Pathology

Providing clinical applications and diagnostic imaging expertise.

Facilities & Infrastructure

We acknowledge the support of:

  • UW School of Medicine facilities and management
  • Engineering and IT support teams
  • Campus safety and environmental health professionals
  • Imaging equipment manufacturers and vendors

Staff & Leadership

We thank our dedicated staff members and leadership who ensure the facility operates at the highest standards, maintaining equipment, providing training, and supporting ongoing research.

✓ Collaborative Spirit

The success of the Keck Center depends on the collaborative spirit of our users, technical staff, and supporting departments. We are committed to fostering an inclusive and productive research environment for all.

Contributing Researchers

We would also like to recognize all the researchers whose work has advanced the field of microscopy and imaging science, and whose research continues to benefit from the facilities and resources provided by the Keck Center.

📞 Questions or Contributions?

If you believe you should be acknowledged or have information to share about the Center's history and support, please contact us at uwkeckcenter@uw.edu
