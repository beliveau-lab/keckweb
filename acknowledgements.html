How to Acknowledge the Keck Center

✓ General Acknowledgement

Please acknowledge the W. M. Keck Microscopy Center in any publication relying on:

  • Imaging data acquired in the Keck Center
  • Training and assistance provided by the Keck Center Manager
  • Image analysis performed in the Keck Center

Suggested General Acknowledgement

"We thank the W. M. Keck Microscopy Center at the University of Washington for access to imaging facilities and assistance with image acquisition/analysis."

👥 Co-authorship Consideration

A discussion of co-authorship may be appropriate if the Keck Center Manager personally conducts critical imaging for a publication (i.e., fully assisted imaging). Please contact the Keck Center Manager to discuss this possibility.

Please give the Keck Center Manager a reprint or reference to any publications acknowledging the Keck Center and its director and manager. This helps us track the impact of our facility.

🔬 Equipment-Specific Acknowledgements

Leica SP8X Confocal Microscope

In addition to the general acknowledgement above, publications using imaging data from the Leica SP8X confocal must also acknowledge:

  • The NIH S10 grant that originally funded the instrument: S10 OD016240
  • The UW Student Technology Fee (UWSTF), which funded computer, scanning hardware, and deconvolution software upgrades

Example: "This work was supported in part by the National Institutes of Health grant S10 OD016240 and the University of Washington Student Technology Fee."

IMARIS Image Analysis Software

In addition to the general acknowledgement above, publications using IMARIS image analysis on the Keck Center's workstation must also acknowledge:

  • The UW Student Technology Fee (UWSTF), which funded hardware and full software upgrades

Example: "Image analysis was performed using IMARIS software at the University of Washington W. M. Keck Microscopy Center, supported in part by the University of Washington Student Technology Fee."

📋 Citation Format Guidelines

Journal Articles & Peer-Reviewed Papers

"We thank the W. M. Keck Microscopy Center at the University of Washington for access to [specific equipment] and assistance with image acquisition and analysis. This work was supported in part by [specific funding if applicable]."

Presentations & Posters

Include a mention in your methods slide or section: "Microscopy performed at the W. M. Keck Microscopy Center, University of Washington"

Theses & Dissertations

Include acknowledgement in the acknowledgements section. You may also add a note in the methods describing which equipment was used.

📊 Publication Tracking

To help us demonstrate the impact of the Keck Center to funding agencies and university administration, please send us a copy or link to your publication that acknowledges the Keck Center.
