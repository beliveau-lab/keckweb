ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Acknowledgements

Funding sources, partner departments, and the people who make the Center possible

Funding & foundational support

The W. M. Keck Microscopy Center is made possible through the generous support of the W. M. Keck Foundation, whose original gift funded the establishment of the facility and continues to guide its mission of advancing microscopy research and education at the University of Washington.

Grant acknowledgement — required for publications

All publications that include data acquired on the Leica SP8X Confocal must acknowledge NIH grant S10 OD016240.

The Leica SP8X scan head electronics, CUDA workstation, and LASX Expert software (including Lightning Deconvolution) were funded by the UW Student Technology Fee (UWSTF). The IMARIS analysis workstation is also UWSTF-funded.

Partner departments

The Keck Center is supported by and serves researchers from across the UW School of Medicine and affiliated programs.

Neurobiology & Biophysics

Leading contributors to advanced microscopy research and training programs at the Keck Center.

Pharmacology

Active users and supporters of cutting-edge imaging methodologies for drug discovery and cellular biology research.

Genome Sciences

Driving innovation in molecular imaging, single-molecule techniques, and biological visualization.

Laboratory Medicine & Pathology

Providing clinical applications and diagnostic imaging expertise for histological and pathological studies.

Staff & users

The success of the Keck Center depends on our dedicated staff — who maintain equipment, provide training, and support ongoing research — and on the collaborative spirit of all users. We are committed to maintaining an inclusive and productive research environment.
