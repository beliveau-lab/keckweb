EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATIONS

Leica DMI6000 Widefield

Inverted Microscope System

System Specifications

Microscope Platform

Type
Inverted widefield research microscope with motorized stage
Objective Turret
6-position motorized turret with automatic nosepiece
Available Objectives
63x 1.40 NA Oil, 40x 1.30 NA Oil, 32x 0.40 NA air, 20x 0.50 NA air, 10x and 5x

Optical Components

Illumination
LED illumination system with transmitted light and epifluorescence
Filter Sets
Full set of fluorescence filter cubes (DAPI, GFP, DsRed, Cy5, and more)
DIC Optics
Differential interference contrast (DIC) for enhanced contrast imaging
Condenser
Motorized, oil immersion capable with numerical aperture up to 1.4

Stage & Motorization

XY Stage
Motorized stage with 76×26 mm scan area and high precision positioning
Z-Axis
Motorized fine focus with stepper motor for automated z-stacks
Incubation System
Compatible with heated stage and environmental chambers for live-cell imaging

Camera System

Detector
High-sensitivity cooled CCD or sCMOS camera for widefield epifluorescence
Acquisition Software
Leica LAS X for image capture and processing

Applications

  • Large area imaging and mosaic tiling
  • Multi-point experiments on fixed samples
  • Live-cell imaging with temperature control
  • High-throughput screening
  • Phase contrast and DIC imaging

Best for: Large-scale surveys and multi-point imaging experiments
Contact: Request training or schedule time
