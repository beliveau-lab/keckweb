EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATIONS

Leica SP8X

Confocal Microscopy System

System Specifications

Optical Configuration

Scan Head
Hybrid confocal scanner with resonant and galvanometric mirrors
Detector Types
HyD Detectors (hybrid photodetectors), PMT (photomultiplier tubes), Standard PMT
Laser Lines
405nm (diode), 458nm, 488nm, 561nm, 633nm solid-state lasers
Objective Lenses
63x 1.40 NA Oil, 40x 1.30 NA Oil, 25x 0.95 NA Water, 10x and 5x air objectives

Image Acquisition

Spatial Resolution
XY: ~200 nm (lateral), Z: ~700 nm (axial) at 405 nm with 63x 1.40 NA objective
Scan Speed
Up to 8000 Hz line scan rate with resonant scanner
Pinhole
Electronically adjustable, ranging from 0.5-600 μm
Maximum Field of View
512×512 to 2048×2048 pixels

Software & Processing

Control Software
Leica LAS X with Lightning deconvolution
Real-time Processing
HyVolution for improved signal and resolution

Environmental Control
Stage
Motorized XY stage with temperature control option
Focus
Motorized Z-drive with definite focus for live-cell imaging
