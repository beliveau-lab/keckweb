EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATIONS

Environmental Control Systems

Live-Cell Imaging Solutions

System Specifications

Pecon Stage-top Incubator

Type
Pecon stage-top incubation system for microscope stages
Temperature Control
Stable temperature control from room temperature to 42°C
Humidity Control
Active humidity regulation to maintain physiological conditions
Gas Control
CO₂ monitoring and control (5%) for pH buffering
Optical Properties
Transparent cover for optimal imaging without light loss

Warner Incubation Systems

Dish/Chamber Options
Warner 35 mm and 40 mm perfusion dish holders
Temperature Range
Room temperature to 40°C with precision ±0.5°C control
Perfusion Capability
Compatible with perfusion systems for drug delivery and medium exchange
Material
Stainless steel construction for durability and sterilizability

Capabilities

  • Long-term live-cell imaging for hours to days
  • Physiological temperature and pH maintenance
  • Reduced phototoxicity through environmental stabilization
  • Compatible with confocal and widefield microscopes
  • Drug application and washout protocols
  • Time-lapse and multi-position experiments

Technical Specifications

Heating Power
Efficient heating element for rapid temperature equilibration
Humidity Maintenance
Prevents sample evaporation during extended imaging sessions
Noise Level
Minimal vibration to ensure high-quality time-lapse imaging
Compatibility
Works with oil immersion and water immersion objectives

Applications

  • Cellular dynamics and intracellular trafficking studies
  • Real-time gene expression imaging
  • Neuronal activity recording with calcium imaging
  • Cell migration and wound-healing assays
  • Organ-on-chip and microfluidic device imaging
  • Pharmacological response assessment

Essential for: Live-cell imaging and long-term time-lapse experiments
