Specifications

Zeiss LSM 880 — detailed specs

Zeiss LSM 880 Confocal Microscope
Laser sources
  • 405 nm diode laser
  • 458 nm, 488 nm, 514 nm Argon laser
  • 561 nm DPSS laser
  • 633 nm HeNe laser
Scanning & detection
  • 32-channel GaAsP spectral detector for simultaneous spectral imaging
  • 2× PMT detectors for standard fluorescence channels
  • Airyscan detector — 1.7× resolution improvement over diffraction limit
  • Bi-directional scanning up to 8 fps at 512×512
  • Transmitted light detector for DIC/brightfield
Airyscan super-resolution
  • ~140 nm lateral resolution, ~400 nm axial resolution
  • Improved signal-to-noise ratio compared to conventional confocal
  • Compatible with all laser lines and standard fluorophores
Software & analysis
  • ZEN Black — acquisition, processing, and analysis
  • Spectral unmixing for multi-fluorophore experiments
  • FRAP, FRET, and time-series modules
  • 3D rendering and orthogonal projection
Objectives available
  • 10×/0.45 air — large field of view, tissue sections
  • 20×/0.80 air — standard fluorescence
  • 40×/1.30 oil — high-resolution subcellular
  • 63×/1.40 oil — maximum resolution, single-molecule imaging
