Imaging Systems

Microscopy platforms

Four complementary imaging systems covering confocal, widefield, and live-cell applications. All require advance reservation and completed training.

Confocal — Leica SP8X

White light laser (470–670 nm), resonant scanner, HyD detectors, Lightning deconvolution. NIH-funded via S10 OD016240.

Confocal — Zeiss LSM 880

32-channel GaAsP spectral detector for real-time linear unmixing of closely overlapping fluorophores.

Leica Inverted Widefield

Motorized stage for mosaic tiling, DIC optics, full fluorescence filter sets. Ideal for large-area and multi-point experiments.

Specifications

Leica SP8X — detailed specs

Grant acknowledgement — required

All publications using data from the Leica SP8X Confocal must acknowledge NIH grant S10 OD016240. Scan head electronics, CUDA workstation, and LASX Expert software were funded by the UW Student Technology Fee (UWSTF).

Leica SP8X Confocal Microscope NIH S10 OD016240
Laser sources
  • White Light Laser (WLL) — continuously tunable 470–670 nm
  • 405 nm, 458 nm, 476 nm, 488 nm, 496 nm, 514 nm (fixed)
Scanning & detection
  • Resonant scanner (8,000 Hz) for rapid live-cell imaging
  • Galvo scanner for high-resolution imaging
  • 3× PMT amplification detectors
  • 2× HyD detectors with time-gating for lifetime measurements
  • Trans-PMT detector for simultaneous DIC/brightfield
Software & analysis
  • LASX Expert (UWSTF-funded) — Lightning deconvolution, FRAP, FRET, spectral unmixing
  • CUDA-enabled GPU workstation for accelerated image processing
Objectives available
  • 10×/0.40 air — large field of view, tissue sections
  • 20×/0.75 air — standard fluorescence
  • 40×/1.30 oil — high-resolution subcellular
  • 63×/1.40 oil — maximum resolution single-molecule
