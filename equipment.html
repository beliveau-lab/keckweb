Equipment

Microscopy systems, environmental controls, and image analysis workstations

Overview

The Keck Center houses four imaging systems, two environmental control units, and a dedicated image analysis workstation. All instruments require advance reservation through our online scheduling system.

SP8X
Confocal — Leica SP8X

White light laser, resonant scanner, HyD detectors with lifetime gating, and Lightning deconvolution for high-resolution 3D imaging.

Specifications
Z880
Confocal — Zeiss LSM 880

32-channel GaAsP spectral detector for real-time spectral unmixing, with pulsed lasers for photoactivation and ablation.

Specifications
WF
Widefield — Leica Inverted DMI6000

Motorized stage for mosaic tiling, DIC and phase contrast optics, and full fluorescence filter sets for rapid multi-channel imaging.

Specifications
ENV
Environmental Control Systems

Pecon stage-top incubator and Warner 35 mm dish incubator for long-term live-cell imaging with temperature and CO₂ control.

Book incubator
IMS
Image Analysis — IMARIS

Full-license 3D/4D analysis suite on a high-performance AMD Threadripper workstation. Free for UW students.

Specifications
Grant acknowledgement

All publications using data from the Leica SP8X Confocal must acknowledge NIH grant S10 OD016240. The scan head electronics, CUDA workstation, and LASX Expert software were funded by the UW Student Technology Fee.

Leica SP8X Confocal

Microscope Base & Stages
  • Leica DMI6000 inverted microscope base
  • Motorized XY stage — mosaic tiling and multi-point live imaging
  • Motorized Z-galvo stage for rapid axial movement
  • Adaptive Focus Control (AFC) for focus maintenance during tiling and live imaging
Objectives
Dry
  • 10×, 20×
Oil Immersion
  • 40×, 63×
Water Immersion
  • 25×, 63×
Laser Lines & Scanning
Tunable
  • 470–670 nm White Light Laser (WLL)
Fixed wavelength
  • 405, 458, 476, 488, 496, 514 nm
Scanning
  • Resonant scanner for rapid live imaging
  • Trans-PMT detector for DIC imaging
Detectors
  • 3× PMT amplification detectors
  • 2× photon-counting HyD detectors with lifetime gating
Software & Advanced Systems UWSTF-funded
  • STP8000 backlit touchpad
  • KLONDIKE scan head electronics
  • Expert CUDA Workstation
  • Leica LASX Expert — Navigator, FRAP, FRET, Lightning Deconvolution modules

Leica Inverted Widefield DMI6000

Microscope Base & Optics
  • Leica DMI6000 inverted base
  • DIC (Differential Interference Contrast) optics
  • Phase contrast optics (long working distance objectives)
Objectives
Dry / Long Working Distance
  • 5× LWD, 10× LWD, 20×, 20× LWD, 40× LWD
Oil Immersion
  • 40×, 100×
Water Immersion
  • 63×
Fluorescence Filters & Automation
Filter cubes
  • BFP, CFP, GFP, YFP, TexasRed, CY5
Automation
  • Motorized filters, shutters, and Z-drive
  • Motorized stage for mosaic tiling and multi-point live imaging
Cameras
  • Leica DFC365 FX CCD — fluorescence, brightfield, DIC, phase contrast
  • Leica MC170HD Color camera — histological stains (crystal violet, H&E, etc.)

IMARIS Image Analysis Workstation

Workstation Hardware UWSTF-funded
  • Puget Systems AMD Threadripper TRX40 ATX
  • 24-core processor
  • 4× Crucial DDR4-3200 32 GB (128 GB total RAM)
  • Samsung Pro and EVO SSD storage
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 8 GB (Founders Edition)
IMARIS Software Modules — Full License
  • Surface, Spot, and Vantage modules for 3D visualization
  • Particle tracking and co-localization analysis
  • XT (Extended Tracking) module
  • Cell module
  • Filament module — neuron and vessel tracing
  • Deconvolution module
  • Batch processing and animation
Pricing

Free for UW students (UWSTF-funded). Image Analysis Rate applies for all other users. Advance reservation required via the scheduling system.

Zeiss LSM 880 Confocal

Scan Head & Detectors
  • Quasar module scan head with 32-channel GaAsP detector
  • Two flanking multialkali PMTs for extended spectral range
  • Real-time spectral unmixing of multiple dyes in a single frame scan
  • Channels optimized for deep-blue and far-red spectrum imaging
  • Liquid-cooled GaAsP and far-red MA PMT — ultra-low noise, enhanced sensitivity
Laser Lines
  • Argon: 458, 488, 514 nm — CFP, GFP, YFP
  • Diode: 561 nm — mCherry, mPlum
  • HeNe: 594 nm — mCherry, mPlum
  • HeNe: 633 nm — far red
  • Pulsed high-power: 405 nm and 440 nm — photoactivation and ablation
Objectives
Dry
  • 5×, 10×, 20×
Oil Immersion
  • 40×, 63×, 100× (no UV)
