Leica SP8X Confocal Specifications

⚠️ All publications that include data acquired on the Leica SP8X confocal must acknowledge NIH grant S10 OD016240.

Microscope Base & Stages

  • Leica DMI6000 inverted microscope
  • Motorized XY stage capable of mosaic tiling and multi-point imaging
  • Motorized Z-galvo stage for rapid Z movement
  • Adaptive focus control (AFC) for maintaining focus during live imaging and/or mosaic tiling

Objectives

  • 10X and 20X dry objectives
  • 40X and 63X oil immersion objectives
  • 25X and 63X water immersion objectives

Laser Lines & Imaging

  • 470-670nm tunable White Light Laser (WLL) system
  • 405, 458, 476, 488, 496, and 514nm fixed wavelength laser lines
  • Resonant scanner for rapid, live-imaging
  • Trans-PMT detector for DIC imaging

Detectors

  • 3 PMT amplification detectors
  • 2 photon-counting HyD detectors with lifetime gating

Advanced Systems & Software (Funded by UWSTF)

  • STP8000 backlit touchpad
  • KLONDIKE scan head electronics
  • Expert CUDA Workstation
  • Leica LASX Expert software with specialized modules:
    • Navigator
    • FRAP (Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching)
    • FRET (Förster Resonance Energy Transfer)
    • Lightning Deconvolution

Note: KLONDIKE scan head electronics, CUDA computer workstation, and LASX Expert software with Lightning deconvolution were funded by the UW Student Technology Fee.

Leica Inverted Multi-Dimensional Widefield Specifications

Microscope Base & Optics

  • Leica DMI6000 inverted microscope
  • DIC (Differential Interference Contrast) optics
  • Phase Contrast optics (for use with long working distance objectives)

Objectives

  • 5X LWD, 10X LWD, 20X, 20X LWD and 40X LWD dry objectives
  • 40X and 100X Oil objectives
  • 63X water objective

Fluorescence & Imaging

  • BFP (blue), CFP (cyan), GFP (green), YFP (yellow), TexasRed (red), and CY5 (far red) filter cubes
  • Motorized filters, shutters, and Z-drive
  • Motorized stage for mosaic tiling and/or multi-point live-imaging

Cameras

  • Leica DFC365 FX CCD camera (fluorescence, brightfield, DIC, phase contrast)
  • Leica MC170HD Color camera (color – e.g., crystal violet, histological stains)

Advanced Imaging Capabilities

  • Z-sectioning
  • Mosaic tiling
  • Multi-point acquisition for live imaging
  • Leica LASX software with Navigator

IMARIS Image Analysis Specifications

Computer Workstation

  • Puget Systems AMD Threadripper TRX40 ATX workstation
  • 24-core processor
  • 4x Crucial DDR4-3200 32GB RAM (128GB total)
  • Samsung Pro and EVO SSD hard drives
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 8GB Founders Edition graphics card

IMARIS Software Modules

Zeiss LSM 880 Confocal Specifications

Scanhead with Advanced Detection

  • Quasar module scanhead with 32-channel GaAsP detector
  • Two flanking multialkali PMTs for extended spectral range
  • 32-channel spectral detector allows real-time unmixing of multiple dyes from a single frame scan
  • Two additional channels optimized for:
    • Deep blue spectrum imaging
    • Far-red spectrum imaging
  • Liquid-cooled spectral GaAsP and far-red MA PMT for ultra-low noise and enhanced sensitivity

Laser Lines

  • Argon laser: 458, 488, 514 nm (CFP, GFP, YFP)
  • Diode laser: 561 nm (mCherry, mPlum)
  • HeNe laser: 633 nm (far red)
  • HeNe laser: 594 nm (mCherry, mPlum)
  • Pulsed high-power lasers: 405 nm and 440 nm (ablation and photoactivation)

Objectives

  • 5X, 10X and 20X dry objectives
  • 40X, 63X, and 100X (no UV) oil immersion objectives
