Welcome to the Keck Microscopy Center

The Keck Microscopy Center at the University of Washington is a state-of-the-art facility dedicated to advancing microscopy research and education. We provide access to cutting-edge imaging equipment, expert technical support, and comprehensive training programs for researchers across the university.

Our Capabilities

We offer:

  • Advanced confocal and widefield microscopy systems
  • Professional image analysis and processing
  • Expert training and consultation
  • Research support and collaboration
  • Technical support and troubleshooting
  • Equipment booking and scheduling
