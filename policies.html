Policies & Guidelines

Please review our policies and guidelines to understand the rules for using the Center:

Important Policies

  • Safety Plan: All users must comply with our safety protocols. This includes proper handling of biological samples, chemical safety, and electrical safety.
  • Sample Preparation: Follow recommended preparation standards for optimal results. Improper sample preparation can damage equipment and compromise imaging quality.
  • Equipment Training: Training is required before independent use of instruments. All new users must complete a training session with a Keck Center staff member.
  • Data Management: Follow data handling and storage guidelines. Users are responsible for backing up their own data.
  • Cancellation Policy: Please provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling of booked time slots.
  • Equipment Care: Users must treat all equipment with care. Any damage due to misuse may result in billing for repairs.

Safety Protocols

The Keck Center maintains strict safety standards to protect users and equipment. All users must:

  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) when required
  • Report any equipment malfunctions immediately to staff
  • Follow all biosafety procedures for biological samples
  • Keep the facility clean and organized
  • Never attempt to repair equipment yourself

User Responsibilities

Users are expected to:

  • Arrive on time for scheduled sessions
  • Contact the center if you need to reschedule or cancel
  • Complete instrument training before using equipment independently
  • Maintain accurate records of your usage for billing purposes
  • Report any problems or concerns to Keck Center staff
