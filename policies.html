📋 Center Policies

Guidelines and regulations for responsible facility use

Home > Policies

General Guidelines

Users are expected to operate equipment responsibly and respectfully. Considerate use is required to maintain access privileges.

✓ Training Requirement

All users must complete training with the Keck Center Manager before using any equipment. Untrained users are not permitted to operate equipment.

Equipment Care & Maintenance

Users must clean the working area after they are finished imaging. This includes:

  • Cleaning objectives and slides
  • Wiping down counter tops
  • Covering the microscope
  • Removing all personal materials from the work area

Biological Materials & Safety

Users MUST inform the Keck Center Manager of any planned live imaging experiments and any health risks associated with work done in the Center.

Imaging of BSL-2 samples requires specific approval from the Keck Center Manager and appropriate modifications to your lab's Biological Use Authorization (BUA).

Recharge Center Status

The Keck Center operates as a UW recharge center. Rates are approved through the Office of Management Accounting and Analysis (MAA) and the Dean's Office. Users are expected to follow all UW financial policies.

💻 Computer & Data Policy

UW Usage Guidelines

Keck Center computers and telephones are subject to UW usage guidelines. Users are responsible for following these guidelines when using this equipment. Please review the UW Appropriate Use Policy.

Data Management

⚠️ Important Data Guidelines

  • Users should not store data indefinitely on Keck Center computers. The Keck Center does not take responsibility for stored user data.
  • Sensitive imaging data must immediately be removed from Keck Center computers after image acquisition.
  • Users are responsible for backing up and removing their data promptly following imaging
  • Labs and associated users are responsible for backup and storage of raw (original) imaging data

📅 Reservation Policy

Booking System

Reservations for using the equipment are made on the Equipment Sign-Up Page on a first-come, first-served basis. Users are expected to respect the reservations of other users and finish imaging/file transfers/cleanup by the end of their online reservation time.

Adherence to Schedule

Users should adhere to reservation schedule as closely as possible and contact the Keck Center Manager if there are any last-minute changes of plan.

📌 Reservation Guidelines

  • Lateness of 1 hour or more may result in cancellation of the reservation
  • No-shows that keep other users blocked from reserving time may be charged for the time they reserved
  • Please be respectful of other users' time allocations
  • If you need to cancel, do so as early as possible
