Center Policies

Guidelines and regulations for responsible facility use

Facility Use Guidelines

The Keck Center is committed to providing a safe, productive, and respectful environment for all users. Please review and adhere to the following policies when using our facilities.

General Policies

  • Access Requirements: All users must be authorized and trained before accessing microscopy equipment.
  • Booking System: Equipment must be reserved in advance through our online scheduling system.
  • Safety Training: Mandatory safety orientation required for all new users.
  • Sample Preparation: Samples must meet our preparation standards for optimal results.
  • Equipment Maintenance: Users are responsible for basic maintenance and equipment care.
  • Cost Recovery: Usage fees apply according to departmental recharge rates.

💡 Tip: Best Practices for Microscopy

Always start with the lowest magnification to locate your sample, then increase magnification as needed. This helps prevent damage to your samples and extends the life of our objective lenses.

Safety Requirements

Safety is our top priority. All users must:

  • Wear appropriate clothing and closed-toe shoes in the lab
  • Know the location of emergency equipment
  • Report any equipment malfunctions immediately
  • Never operate equipment if you feel unsafe or unsure
  • Follow all electrical safety guidelines

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

Never leave imaging equipment unattended while it is operating. Always power down equipment at the end of your session and notify staff of any damage or concerns.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • 24+ hours notice: Free cancellation or rescheduling
  • 6-24 hours notice: 25% cancellation fee
  • Less than 6 hours: 50% cancellation fee
  • No-show: 100% booking fee charged
