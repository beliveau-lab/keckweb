Services & Rates

Billing, services offered, and how to get a rate quote

Billing

The Keck Center operates as a UW recharge center. Billing is based on actual instrument usage time. Rates vary by instrument and by user affiliation — UW student, UW staff/postdoc, UW faculty, or external (non-UW) user.

For current hourly rates, contact us directly. We're happy to provide a project estimate before you begin.

Request a rate quote
IMARIS pricing

The IMARIS image analysis workstation is free for UW students, funded by the UW Student Technology Fee. The standard Image Analysis Rate applies for all other users.

Services offered

  • Sample preparation consultation
  • Equipment operation training (individual and group)
  • Assisted image acquisition
  • Image analysis support — IMARIS, Fiji/ImageJ, and custom workflows
  • Experimental design consultation
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • External collaborations — contact us to discuss arrangements

External users

We welcome researchers from institutions outside the University of Washington. Please contact us to discuss your project needs, instrument availability, and applicable rates.
