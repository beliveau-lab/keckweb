Services & Rates

The Keck Center bills based on actual instrument usage time. We offer competitive hourly rates and flexible billing options.

For detailed pricing information, contact us:
📧 Email: uwkeckcenter@uw.edu

Services Offered

  • Sample preparation consultation
  • Microscope training and support
  • Image acquisition assistance
  • Image analysis support
  • Technical consultation

Billing Information

The Keck Center operates as a UW recharge center. Billing is based on actual usage time of the equipment. Rates vary depending on the specific instrument and the user's affiliation (UW student, UW staff, external user).

For questions about billing or to request a rate quote for your project, please contact us at uwkeckcenter@uw.edu
