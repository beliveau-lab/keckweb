Services & Rates

Billing, services offered, and how to get a rate quote

Billing

The Keck Center operates as a UW recharge center. Billing is based on actual instrument usage time. Rates vary by instrument and by user type — UW users or external (industry / for-profit) users.

Hourly rates

Approved by Management Accounting and Analysis (MAA) and the Dean’s Office for 11/1/25 – 10/31/26.

Services offered

  • Sample preparation consultation
  • Equipment operation training (individual and group)
  • Assisted image acquisition
  • Image analysis support — IMARIS, Fiji/ImageJ, and custom workflows
  • Experimental design consultation
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • External collaborations — contact us to discuss arrangements

External users

We welcome researchers from institutions outside the University of Washington. Please contact us to discuss your project needs, instrument availability, and applicable rates.
