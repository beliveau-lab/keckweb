Visiting the Center

Before your first visit, please:

  • Contact us to schedule a time
  • Discuss your imaging needs with our staff
  • Arrange for training if you're a new user
  • Prepare your samples according to our guidelines

We're Here to Help

Whether you have questions about our equipment, need assistance with sample preparation, or want to discuss your research project, the Keck Center team is ready to help.
