Shipping & deliveries

Small packages & mail

Mail and small shipments that can be carried should go to the Neurobiology and Biophysics main office, not to the Keck Center.

University of Washington
Dept. of Neurobiology and Biophysics
Health Sciences Building G-424, Box 357290
Seattle, WA 98195

Large deliveries

Large deliveries, such as microscope systems in shipping crates, go directly to the Keck Center via the K-wing loading dock. Please contact the Keck Center Manager before shipping any items to the Center.

K-wing Loading Dock
3500 NE Columbia Road
Seattle, WA 98195
