🧬 Sample Preparation & Protocols

Standard Sample Preparation

Proper sample preparation is crucial for high-quality imaging. Consider the following:

  • Fixation: Use appropriate fixatives for your specimen type
  • Mounting: Choose mounting media based on your fluorophores and imaging method
  • Coverslips: Use appropriate thickness (#1.5 for most applications)
  • Artifacts: Be aware of common imaging artifacts and how to minimize them

Contact the Keck Center Manager for specific protocol recommendations for your research.

🎓 Training Resources

Getting Started at the Keck Center

All users must complete training before operating equipment. Training sessions cover:

  • Equipment operation and safety procedures
  • Sample handling and preparation
  • Image acquisition best practices
  • Data management and storage
  • Center policies and scheduling

Contact us: uwkeckcenter@uw.edu to schedule your training session.
