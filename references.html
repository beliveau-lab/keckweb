REFERENCE MATERIALS

Reference Materials

Guides, protocols, software resources, and training materials for facility users

Getting started

Whether you are new to the facility or looking to expand your imaging repertoire, these materials are a starting point. Contact us for instrument-specific documentation or to request a custom protocol.

Fundamentals

Microscopy Fundamentals Guide

Introduction to light microscopy principles, optical sectioning, and fluorescence. Recommended reading before a first training session.

Protocols

Sample Preparation Handbook

Fixation, staining, and mounting protocols for common biological samples. Includes recommendations for each instrument in the facility.

Equipment

Instrument-Specific Tutorials

Step-by-step setup guides for the Leica SP8X, Zeiss 880, Leica Widefield, and IMARIS workstation — including acquisition parameter templates.

Advanced

Advanced Imaging Techniques

Resources on FRAP, FRET, spectral unmixing, live-cell timelapse, and mosaic tiling strategies.

Analysis

Image Analysis Guide

Tutorials for IMARIS (3D/4D), Fiji/ImageJ, and common analysis workflows including segmentation, particle tracking, and co-localization.

Data Management

Data Management Best Practices

File naming conventions, metadata standards, directory structure recommendations, and archival guidance for microscopy datasets.

Software resources

  • IMARIS — Bitplane 3D/4D visualization and quantitative analysis suite (full license; free for UW students)
  • Fiji / ImageJ — Free, open-source image processing platform with extensive plugin ecosystem
  • Leica LASX — Leica microscope control and image acquisition, including Lightning Deconvolution
  • Zeiss ZEN — Zeiss instrument control, with spectral unmixing module
Good practice

Calibrate your microscope before each imaging session and document your acquisition parameters thoroughly. Consistent metadata greatly improves reproducibility and enables accurate comparisons across experiments.

External resources

  • UW Libraries — Research Guides
  • Microscopy Society of America (MSA) educational resources
  • Manufacturer documentation and webinar libraries (Leica, Zeiss, Bitplane)
  • Peer-reviewed microscopy journals: Journal of Microscopy, Nature Methods, Current Protocols in Cell Biology

Training sessions

The Keck Center offers individual and group training on all facility instruments, image analysis workflows, and troubleshooting. We can also develop custom protocols for specific research applications.

Request training

To schedule a training session or request specific documentation, contact us at uwkeckcenter@uw.edu.
